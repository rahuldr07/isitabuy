# Product Strategy MOC

Use this map when changing product direction, UX copy, information hierarchy, or feature scope.

## Product Promise

IsItABuy helps shoppers decide whether a product is worth buying now. The experience should feel independent, evidence-based, and fast.

Core verdicts:

- Buy - price, quality, reviews, and alternatives support purchasing now.
- Wait - product is good, but timing or price is not ideal.
- Avoid - risk, quality, fake reviews, price, or better alternatives make it a poor choice.
- Better Alternative - a stronger product exists for the user's goals or budget.

## Target User Jobs

- "Tell me if this product is actually worth it."
- "Show me whether the price is good right now."
- "Warn me about review manipulation or hidden quality issues."
- "Find a better alternative if this one is not the best value."
- "Track it and alert me when the price makes sense."
- "Check whether my recent purchase can be refunded or price-matched."

## Current Experience Pillars

- Search by product name or URL.
- Product verdict cards with score breakdowns.
- Product detail page for deeper evidence.
- Comparison flow for side-by-side decisions.
- Deals and price tracking pages.
- Watchlist and price alert pages.
- Receipt upload and analysis concept.
- AI concierge/chat concept.
- Preferences page for personalization.

## UX Principles

- Lead with the verdict, then explain the evidence.
- Make the "why" scannable: price, review trust, quality, alternatives.
- Keep commission and affiliate policy transparent.
- Avoid implying real backend intelligence until the feature exists.
- Prefer focused shopping tasks over generic AI chatbot behavior.

## Product Risks

- The UI currently presents static demo data as if it were dynamic intelligence.
- Affiliate messaging is inconsistent with "commission-free" positioning.
- Several route concepts overlap and may confuse navigation.
- Search, filters, chat, receipt processing, and alerts are mostly presentational.
